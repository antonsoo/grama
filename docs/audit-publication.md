Public browser checks operate both lessons in Chromium and Firefox at 1440 and
375 px: real controls, fixed fourth/fifth lock, failed candidates, actual live
PCM, JSON/CSV/WAV/HTML downloads and reopening, all eleven source identities,
keyboard navigation and standalone script-free readings. The saved audio and
reading bytes agree with their online counterparts when browser networking is
disabled after installation. The public origin remains reachable; this scope is
separate from the local tests that actually shut down the origin. Source/cache
fault injection and guaranteed unavailable-origin recovery are local checks,
not claims inferred from a public browser's offline toggle.

The public discovery route starts at Officina's **Bharata** search, opens the
count bench and follows **Find another tuning**. The learner adds ten cents to
the two-count gap while keeping the initial fourth and fifth pure, verifies the
printed editorial uncertainty page, downloads editable JSON, and inspects the
immutable audit. The catalogue's older evidence link remains pinned to its
original count-bench review; it is not rewritten to imply that it reviewed the
later family.
